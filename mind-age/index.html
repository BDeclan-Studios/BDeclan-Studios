About

Mind Age is a brain-training game. Each day, take a quick Brain Check of four short tests (or choose three or five in Settings) and get your Mind Age, from 20 to 80. Then keep sharp with training games for maths, memory, speed and puzzles.

Track your Mind Age over time, see a score out of 100 for each skill area, collect a stamp for every day you train, earn achievements, get a weekly summary every Sunday, and compare best scores with friends on Game Center. Miss a day? One streak freeze a week keeps your streak going. Games start harder once you're scoring well, and questions, words and puzzles change every time you play.

Made for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad, in light or dark mode. On iPhone Duo, open your phone for a two-page notebook layout, and unfold it to reveal your Mind Age. On iPhone Duo or iPad, play head-to-head against a friend on the same device.

Mind Age is a game for fun and practice. It is not a medical test and does not diagnose anything.

How is my Mind Age worked out?

Each test scores how quickly and accurately you play. Your scores are averaged and turned into an age between 20 (best) and 80.

Can I do more than one Brain Check a day?

The Brain Check is once a day, so your chart shows real day-to-day progress. You can play the training games as often as you like.

How do I turn reminders or sounds on or off?

Open Settings from the home screen. You can set a daily reminder time, turn the weekly summary, sounds and haptics on or off, choose light or dark mode, and pick three, four or five tests for your Brain Check.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and tap Reset all progress. If iCloud is on, this also clears your progress from iCloud and your other devices. Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple.

Will my progress carry over to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on. Mind Age saves your progress and settings to your own iCloud, so it carries over to a new device or if you reinstall the app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Mind Age is made by Brodie Campbell (BDStudios). We don't collect any personal data ourselves. The free version shows banner ads from Google, which collects some data as described below.

  • No account or sign-in required.
  • Your scores, Mind Age history, settings, and the name and age you can optionally enter stay on your device. If you use iCloud, they are also saved to your own private iCloud storage so they sync across your devices. We can't see this data, and we don't run any servers.
  • To avoid repeating questions, the app remembers which words, puzzles and passages you've seen recently. This list stays on your device.
  • Game Center is used for leaderboards and achievements, managed by Apple.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.
  • Daily reminders and the weekly summary are scheduled on your device, and only if you allow notifications.
  • Emails you send for support are used only to reply to you.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads between the game sections on the Home screen. Ads never appear during a game or your Daily Brain Check, and Mind Age Pro and Family subscribers never see ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location, such as your country or city;
  • device and app information, such as model, iOS version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you've agreed, personalise ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: if you choose "Ask App Not to Track" when iOS asks, your advertising identifier isn't shared. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The UK and Switzerland: before any ads are shown, you'll be asked for consent with Google's consent form. You can change your choice later with Ad privacy choices in Mind Age's Settings.
  • No ads at all: Mind Age Pro removes ads completely.

Mind Age is a general-audience game, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from children.
