About

Mind Age is a brain-training game. Each day, take a quick Brain Check of three short tests and get your Mind Age, from 20 to 80. Then keep sharp with training games for maths, memory, speed and puzzles.

Track your Mind Age over time, collect a stamp for every day you train, and compare best scores with friends on Game Center.

Optimised for iPhone and iPhone Duo. On iPhone Duo, open your phone for a two-page notebook layout, and unfold it to reveal your Mind Age.

Mind Age is a game for fun and practice. It is not a medical test and does not diagnose anything.

How is my Mind Age worked out?

Each test scores how quickly and accurately you play. Your three scores are averaged and turned into an age between 20 (best) and 80.

Can I do more than one Brain Check a day?

The Brain Check is once a day, so your chart shows real day-to-day progress. You can play the training games as often as you like.

How do I turn reminders or sounds on or off?

Open Settings from the home screen. You can set a daily reminder time, and turn sounds and haptics on or off.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and tap Reset all progress. Progress is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app also clears it. Game Center scores are kept by Apple.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Mind Age does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in required.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising.
  • No data sent to any server.
  • Your scores, Mind Age history, and the name and age you can optionally enter stay on your device.
  • Game Center is used only for leaderboards, managed by Apple.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.
  • Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone and only if you turn them on.
  • Emails you send for support are used only to reply to you.

Mind Age is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children, or from anyone.
