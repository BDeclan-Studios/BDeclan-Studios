Save Point

Your game library, reviews and friends in one place.

About Support Privacy Policy

About

Save Point keeps track of the games you own, are playing and want to play. Search for any game to add it to your library, see its details and scores, and find games that are coming soon.

Sign in with Apple to post your own reviews and see what other players think, and add friends with a friend code to see what they're playing.

Save Point is free, with small ads in Discover and on game pages, never in your library. A one-off Remove Ads purchase removes them on every device signed in to your Apple ID.

Why do I need to sign in to post a review?

Reviews are public, so posting one needs Sign in with Apple. You can use the rest of the app without signing in.

How do I report a review or a profile?

Use the report option on the review or profile. Reports are checked, and posts that break the rules are removed.

How do I stop sharing my profile?

You can stop sharing your profile at any time. It's removed from the shared database, and friends no longer see it.

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Open Settings, go to Support Save Point, and tap Restore Purchase.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you're in Europe or the UK, open Settings, go to Support Save Point, and tap Ad Privacy Choices. Anywhere, you can turn tracking off in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your shared profile, your friends and friend requests, and every review you've written, and unlinks Save Point from your Apple ID. Your game library isn't affected.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Save Point is made by Brodie Campbell (BDStudios). We don't use analytics. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some data as described below.

  • Your library stays on your device and in your own iCloud account, managed by Apple. We can't see it.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional. We receive an anonymous ID from Apple and, the first time you sign in, your name, which is only used to suggest your display name. We don't ask for your email address.
  • Reviews you post (your rating, review text, display name and platform) are stored in Save Point's public iCloud database, run by Apple, and can be read by every Save Point player. An ID for your iCloud account is stored with each review so we can handle reports and blocking; it is never shown. You can delete your own reviews.
  • Your friend profile is shared only if you choose to. It can include your display name, bio, platforms, favourites, game stats, what you're playing now and a profile picture. It's stored in the same public iCloud database, and anyone with your friend code can find it. Friend requests are stored there too. Stopping sharing removes your profile.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, Apple's iCloud service sends you a notification when someone sends you a friend request or accepts yours.
  • Blocking and reports: people you block are kept in a list on your device. Reports you make send the reported item and your reason to our moderation queue.
  • Game search: what you search for, and which game pages you open, are sent to Save Point's own server, which gets the results from IGDB (owned by Twitch). No personal information is sent with these searches. Game cover images load from IGDB's servers.
  • Purchases (Remove Ads) are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.
  • Deleting your account removes your shared profile, the friend requests you've sent and every review you've written. To unlink Save Point from your Apple ID, the app sends a one-time code from Apple to Save Point's server, which passes it back to Apple to revoke the link. The server doesn't keep it.
  • Emails you send for support are used only to reply to you.

Advertising

The free version shows small, labelled ads in Discover and on game pages. There are never ads in your library, on profiles or on friends screens, and there are no ads at all after you buy Remove Ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location, such as your country or city;
  • device and app information, such as model, iOS version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you've agreed, personalise ads. If you don't allow tracking, ads are still shown but aren't personalised. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: if you choose "Ask App Not to Track" when iOS asks, your advertising identifier isn't shared. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe and the UK: before any ads are shown, you'll be asked for consent with Google's consent form. You can change your choice later with Ad Privacy Choices in the app's Settings.
  • No ads at all: Remove Ads removes ads completely.

Save Point is a general-audience app, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from children.
