How do I play?

Customers order drinks. Stack the right ingredients in the right order to fill each cup. Serve fast, keep your tips up, and unlock new menu packs and decor for your café.

How do I unlock more drinks?

Earn tips by serving customers well. Spend tips in the shop to buy new menu packs, each with new ingredients and drink recipes.

How do I change my café?

Open the shop from the hub screen. You can buy and equip decor items — wallpaper, counters, cups and more — to personalise your café.

How do I reset my progress?

Progress is stored only on your phone. Deleting and reinstalling Stack Café starts it fresh.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Stack Café does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

  • Your progress and settings (day, tips, unlocks, decor, sound and haptics preferences) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me or anyone else.
  • Emails you send for support are used only to reply to you.

Stack Café is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children, or from anyone.
