How do I play?

Just walk with your iPhone or Apple Watch. Step Isles reads your step count from Apple Health and turns it into shells to decorate your island. As you level up, your island grows and new animal friends move in.

What are achievements?

Step Isles has Game Center achievements you can earn by reaching milestones, exploring new islands, and connecting with friends. Check your progress in Game Center.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap the menu button (bottom right), then Shop. Under Decoration packs, tap Restore. Your packs will come back automatically.

The app isn't tracking my steps.

Step Isles reads your steps from Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps > Step Isles, and make sure Steps is turned on. Then reopen Step Isles.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Step Isles does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

  • Step count data is read from HealthKit on your device to power gameplay. This data is never sent to me, any server, or anyone else. It stays on your phone.
  • Your progress and settings (islands unlocked, achievements, preferences) are saved only on your device.
  • Game Center is used for leaderboards and achievements. Your Game Center display name and scores are handled by Apple under their own privacy policy.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. I do not receive any payment details.
  • Emails you send for support are used only to reply to you.

Step Isles is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children, or from anyone.
