How do I play?

Open the phone to see the puzzle page. Fold it shut to hear Mags read one clue on the cover screen. The guard closes in faster while you're folded, so remember what you heard and open up to crack the lock.

I don't have an iPhone Duo.

That's fine. On any other iPhone, hold the walkie-talkie button next to pause and Mags sends you a note without folding anything. You can turn that button off in Settings if you would rather flip the phone instead.

The guard is too fast.

Open Settings from the journal or the pause card and set Guard to Relaxed. You can also make Mags's notes stay on screen longer.

Play Together won't connect.

Both phones need Vault Duo open, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth turned on, and Local Network allowed for Vault Duo in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. Keep the phones near each other.

How do I reset my progress?

Progress is stored only on your phone. Deleting and reinstalling Vault Duo starts it fresh.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Vault Duo does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

  • Your progress and settings (stars, trophies, sound, guard speed and so on) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me or anyone else.
  • Play Together uses your local network to connect two nearby phones directly. Game moves pass only between those two phones and nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Motion and hinge sensors are used only to tell whether the phone is open, folded or face down. That information never leaves the device.
  • Emails you send for support are used only to reply to you.

Vault Duo is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children, or from anyone.
